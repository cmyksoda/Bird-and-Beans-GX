Pyoro voice recordings: Nintendo / Intelligent Systems, from WarioWare: Get It Together! These recordings are not covered by the port's GPLv3 license. No redistribution license from the copyright holders is asserted.

The WAV collection was submitted by Mysticus to The Sounds Resource. The vendored voice pack contains 55 clips, excluding Failure and GameOver entries. Source filenames, selection and per-file checksums are recorded in assets/voices-manifest.json in the project source repository.
